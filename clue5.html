SOLVE THE PIRATE CODE!

In Curse of the Black Pearl, Captain Barbossa and his crew suffer under an ancient Aztec curse!

Movie Quote: "We are cursed men, Miss Turner. Compelled by greed, we took a stone from the stone chest. We took them all!"

The crew stole 882 pieces of Aztec gold. Until every piece is returned with blood repaid, they cannot die, cannot feel - condemned to exist as the undead!

Decode the pirates secret message:
PIRATE CIPHER (First letter of each line)
Cursed by the Aztec gold we took,
Under moonlight, see our skeletal look.
Return the coins with blood to pay,
Sailing forever, we cannot stay.
Every piece must find its place,

Only then will we have grace.
Fear us not, for we are dead,

The Black Pearl is where we're led.
Here we wait for the curse to break,
End this torment for heavens sake!
